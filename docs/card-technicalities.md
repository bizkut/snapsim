# Card and engine technicalities

This is an implementation-oriented reference for the Rust simulator and the Android 59.15.1 client evidence, not a statement that every client rule is implemented or every restored position is exact. Client addresses below are RVAs in that build. The client corpus and binaries are not distributed with this repository. The current registry is `cards::GROUPS` and `cards::TOKEN_GROUPS`; `locations::REGISTRY` is the location registry. For card-by-card descriptions, evidence, and status see [coverage-audit.md](coverage-audit.md). Its summary counts are historical, and individual rows can have been updated since its baseline. `crates/snapsim/src/coverage.rs` is authoritative for the actual position-dependent gates; it changes as the reader and simulator acquire state.

## Turn and ability timing

* Game start runs start-of-game abilities before the opening draw (`StartGame::OnEnter`, 0x7e47c68). On a normal turn the engine refreshes energy, applies scheduled player grants and draws, handles scheduled location reveals and start-of-turn triggers, refreshes Ongoing, then enters staging. Client start and post-start triggers refresh Ongoing at 0x7e4a36c and 0x7e4ab00. Hand and deck observers also receive relevant game-start, start-turn, draw and Empower events.
* Staging is not resolution. A player may stage plays, moves and activations in order; the client only permits undoing the latest staged action (`Card::IsStageUndoable`, 0x7cb0e74). Some effects use the staged lane or previous zone even after the entity moves or leaves play. A staged card can remain unrevealed across turns, so its staging turn is not its reveal turn.
* Priority is the leading player; a tied board uses a random choice. Each player's actions resolve in priority order and then staging order. A card is marked revealed and Ongoing is refreshed before its On Reveal and play observers (`Card::ResolveForPlay`). Delayed reveals are queued; at the game boundary the engine releases remaining face-down cards. Persistent delays use the resolution-player order in `EndTurn::GetOrderedStagedCardsThisGameForResolve` (0x7e0a8e0). A location reveal also refreshes Ongoing (`Location::RevealLocation`, 0x7de100c).
* `AbilitySystem::UpdateOngoingStats` (0x7c94474) orders Init, modifiers, Default, ConditionalCard, ConditionalLocation and PostConditional. The priority passes are **1, 0, 2, 3, 4**. Within a pass, player-owned abilities are visited in attachment order, then locations, then cards; hand Ongoing is also scheduled. Living Tribunal has an explicit before-Omega-Red ordering exception. Failed conditional card and location activations in passes 2 and 3 are retried after successes until no pending activation succeeds (`RerunConditionalOngoingAbilities`, 0x7c982e8).
* A full Ongoing refresh clears and recomputes contributions. During staging the client clears movement/play restriction caches rather than all Ongoing stats (`Card::ClearOngoingStatsWhileStaging`, 0x7cb4cec); the simulator evaluates those restrictions on demand and preserves non-staging contributions. Only abilities marked active during staging run in that mode. This distinction matters for ordering and attribution of stat updates.
* End of turn is not a per-card pre/end pair. `EndTurn::OnEnter` (0x7e09558) runs pre-end first. In the non-reference engine, card pre-end watches/hooks precede the end-turn location hooks, then revealed cards execute in latest-resolution order, followed by eligible hand/deck observers, player effects, an Ongoing refresh, objective freeze, and after-turn events. `Game::ActivateTriggerAbility` (0x7da93a0) visits locations before the revealed-card list; `Game::GetAllRevealedCards` (0x7dac98c; sort key 0x7dc6bfc) uses `LatestResolutionOrder`. Sandcastle and TVA end the game after that turn's effects, not midway through it (0x7d7e0fc, 0x7d8f724).
* Ability state belongs to an ability instance, not just a printed card. Nested re-entry of the same reentrant instance shares its root execution context; a removed instance stays alive until its active frames return. `CheckReentrant` (0x7ca61e8) blocks re-entry of a non-reentrant instance, not nesting of distinct instances. `Ability::CanActivate` (0x7c8a6e0) caps the global active ability stack at 32; player and location triggers count too. Stryfe's borrowed On Reveal executes in a temporary ability context; copying its card retains Stryfe's persistent state rather than that temporary context.

## Copy, text, zones and locations

* `Card::CopyEffects` (0x7caf324) clones attached ability instances and their contexts, including currently executing context. Hidden attached abilities are not the same as an Ongoing ability: Phoenix Force's permanent movable grant survives Isle of Silence. Transient copied Ongoing parents have independent contexts and their child attachments retain the corresponding parent relationship. Objective progress is reset on a card copy; do not treat a copied objective as a clone of completed progress.
* `Card::CopyText` (0x7cbadb4) installs an override with fresh default ability state; it is not a general copy of the other entity's persistent state. `CopyTextAndActivateResolveAbility` (0x7cbb3f4) refreshes Ongoing before activating the selected new default resolve; `ActivateTextResolveAbility` (0x7cbb424) uses the reveal repetition count (0x7cb2d1c), not all foreign attached resolves. `RemoveDefaultAbilities` (0x7cbe110) preserves foreign-owned attachments. Removing text stops its default event reactions, but does not imply that all separately attached abilities disappeared.
* `MorphIntoCopy` (0x7cbb5cc) copies persistent target state and calls CopyEffects; it is distinct from copying only text. Merge transfers power and text and clones eligible attached abilities; the receiver's existing default abilities are not duplicated. `Card::Copy` (0x7caed0c) also carries replacement provenance; clear-override paths clear it. Visual-only attachments are not modelled.
* Cards have explicit zones and previous zones. The ordered graveyard contains both discarded and destroyed cards; these are distinguished by provenance when known. Banish is a zone transition (`Game::BanishCard`, 0x7dac084), not a discard or destroy. Zone history matters to effects such as Leader and Sparky, and a card may leave play before a staged-play observer sees it. `Deck::GetTopCard` (0x7cc0b28) is the literal top entity, not necessarily the next entity a draw operation would take.
* Galactus and Worldship call `Game::DestroyLocation` (0x7daeaf4), which removes the lane before forcibly destroying its occupants. Destroyed lanes are not ordinary empty locations. The adapter needs a complete original three-lane entity layout to reconstruct omitted destroyed locations. Lane changes also affect location-indexed zone references.
* Location suppression is distinct from removing card text. Snowguard Hawk suppresses location abilities (`SilenceLocation`, 0x7f41cd0), including location callbacks, for its tracked lifetime; Isle of Silence disables Ongoing (`DisableOngoing`, 0x7d5a9ec); Deep Space prevents card abilities (`PreventCardAbilities`, 0x7d4e4dc). Knowhere prevents On Reveals (0x7d5d934). These are not interchangeable.

## Mechanics and dependent cards

The lists below name implemented examples with particularly relevant edge cases, not every card in a category. The audit's mechanics column is the per-card inventory, including unsupported cards and evidence status.

### Staging, reveal and repeated abilities

* **Sinister London / Skills:** `SinisterLondonBehavior::CreateCopy` (0x7d7fe8c) can copy a staged Skill after it resolved and was banished; requiring it still to be in a lane is wrong. **Shuri's Lab:** `DoublePower` (0x7d7fb10) keys off where the card was played at PostCardResolved, even if it moved away, not merely where it is now. **Wolfsbane:** `GetTargets` uses `Location::GetAllCards`, including face-down allies. **Galacta:** `BuffCard` (0x7ead88c) uses the first eligible staged play at another lane and per-instance trigger turn.
* **Absorbing Man** uses the last played card only if it is still in play and has a resolve ability (0x7e5ee44). **Chameleon** selects an allied card revealed this turn (0x7e80194). **Stryfe** borrows hand resolves with independent contexts (0x7cca334). **Nico Minoru** has current spell text and a separate preparation trigger. Reveal repetition applies to selected copied default resolve text, not every attached resolve.
* **Invisible Woman**, **Dark Dimension**, and **Malekith** can leave cards concealed or delay their resolve. Concealment staging order, target identity and attached delayed-reveal state can be missing from a restored position. **Zenn-La** recruits the first deck card of printed cost 3 and reveals it; modified cost is not its predicate (0x7d9c414).

### Ongoing, costs and modifiers

* **Cerebro** selects all tied highest-current-power revealed allies (`BuffHighestPowerCard`, 0x7e7f490); **Ajax** counts each afflicted revealed target once, using negative additive modifiers and stat updates (0x7e638f4). **Luke Cage** suppresses applicable negative modifiers rather than deleting their history (`SetCanHavePowerReduced`, 0x7cb8478). **Caiera** checks printed costs 1 and 6 across locations (0x7e79584).
* **Monstro Octopus** reduces its cost by the literal top deck card's cost (`ReduceCost`, 0x7efec0c). A restored hand needs attributable permanent cost updates, complete own deck identities and a top card compatible with observed cost; unknown deck-top cost effects make the basis uncertain. **Baron Mordo** attaches a player-owned cost setter to the target deck card (0x7e6eaac); **Black Swan** uses printed cost (0x7e739f4). Multiple card/player cost effects have attachment order.
* **Electro**, **Jean Grey**, **Thena**, **Infinaut** and **Brooklyn Bridge** depend on staged-play history, not merely the cards currently visible. **Sandman**, **Wave**, **Stick** and **Psylocke** can install player-owned effects surviving the source. **Arishem**, **Corvus Glaive**, **Havok**, **Wiccan**, **Fallen One** and **Cyclops X-Men** depend on energy history rather than current displayed energy alone.

### Movement, destruction, merge and ownership

* **Phoenix Force** resurrects and merges a destroyed card and installs a permanent movable ability (`ReviveAndMergeCard`, 0x7cdb304; `MovablePermanent`, 0x7e101c8); its hidden movement grant is not disabled by Isle of Silence. **SP//dr**, **Miek**, **Cloak**, **Hellion**, **Arana** and Champion Ghost Rider also depend on attached or player-owned movement grants. **Batroc** consumes its once-per-turn trigger only after successful movement (0x7e709f4). **Ghost Spider** and **Toad** use the last play even if that card has since left play (0x7eb473c, 0x7cdf2e8).
* **Blob** merges a selected deck prefix as one batch (`MergeDeck`, 0x7e753e4; `Card::Merge`, 0x7cbbf50). **Symbiote Spider-Man** and **Klyntar** likewise depend on merge and attachment semantics. **Archangel Horseman** counts owner destruction events before resurrection (0x7e697a0, 0x7e697b4). **Bucky Barnes** creates Winter Soldier at the destruction location (0x7e77564).
* **Annihilus** snapshots revealed allies of negative current power, then transfers or destroys on transfer failure (0x7e6522c). **Viper**, **Green Goblin**, **Hobgoblin** and **Acid Arrow** also depend on location ownership-change restrictions (`Location::CanCardChangeOwner`, 0x7ddc3b4). **Cannonball** creates a Rock only if the attempted destruction succeeds (0x7e7a788). **Galactus** requires winning his lane with exactly one allied card before destroying other locations (0x7eae710).

### Created cards, decks, draw and random pools

* **Rama-Tut** executes three distinct borrowed historical resolves (`ActivateResolveAbilities`, 0x7f1ef48). The captured client whitelist yields 107 resolve-capable IDs in `data/past_years_pool.json`; `GetPastYearsCards` is 0x7f1f418. `parity/generate_past_years_pool.py` documents date, exclusion and trigger filters and notes that game configuration can further restrict the captured set. The simulator's implemented-only candidates change the distribution.
* **Arishem** samples 12 distinct definitions outside the starting deck before shuffle and schedules turn-three energy (0x7e6a51c); the game-start observer snapshot excludes newly generated cards (0x7dacfcc). **Agent Coulson** selects the printed-cost 4 and 5 pools before creating either card (0x7e616cc). **Nick Fury** selects three distinct 6-cost definitions and excludes Agatha (0x7f0c2fc). **Agent 13**, **Agamotto**, **Thor**, **Infinity Stones** and locations that shuffle cards depend on pool or deck composition.
* **Yondu** banishes a randomly selected tied lowest-current-cost enemy deck card (0x7cf8068). **Baron Zemo** recruits randomly among tied cheapest opposing deck cards (0x7e6f3fc). **Copycat** reads the opponent's bottom deck on draw and copies text (0x7e838dc). **Ares** compares the first three deck cards' powers (0x7e69e70, 0x7e6a03c). Hidden opponent hands and decks are sampled, not read.
* Client random pools can include unimplemented definitions. Except for explicitly complete curated pools, simulator random card and location creation is limited to implemented content; it does not reproduce the client's RNG stream. The Scarlet Witch Queen of Chaos skill pool is complete (13 curated and 11 collectible skills). Nico's location spell and Omniversal Presence use uniform implemented-location choices although the client weights `GetRandomLocationDefId`.

### Automation, stakes and winnings

* **Ego** stages for both players (`AutoPlay`, 0x7d50618). **Agatha Harkness** draws herself at game start and stages autonomously on even turns, preferring her own valid option before sampling (0x7e60570, 0x7e6091c). `Game::GatherOptions` (0x7da8e90) and `AiPlayDef::Stage` (0x7e10b94) cover play, move, activate and staged-play return. Nondefault `CanBeReturnedToHand` grants are not modelled; restored autonomous staging may lack completion/undo state.
* Snap projection computes `min(maximum, current × player multiplier × end-game multiplier × game multiplier)` (`Game::UpdateCubeValueNextTurn`, 0x7db0df8–0x7db0e2c); promotion uses `CheckIncreaseStakesForNextTurn` (0x7db0fc4), and the configured final multiplier is capped (0x7dccd14). A supplied initial next-stake value is a snapshot, not proof of the underlying multipliers. Concession checks the displayed current stake and result creation copies it (0x7e50204, 0x7dd4324). The last raiser cannot concede on their own snap turn (0x7dd1bf4); deferred concessions precede reveal and promotion (0x7e09570, 0x7e09764); simultaneous concessions tie (0x7dd3a6c). Simulator simultaneous requests are randomly ordered; retreat/draw comparison semantics remain provisional. Mount Vesuvius's retreat prohibition is not modelled.
* **Hydra Bob** checks both players' snap turns (0x7ec8b00). **Mephisto** has no client behavior-map entry for his advertised extra winnings; payout handling is server-side and not modelled. Stake advice declines if observed in play or over 5% of sampled end states, otherwise reports unknown samples separately. Ordinary board advice is unaffected. Stake policies are independent-action sensitivity models, not calibrated opponent behavior.

### Objectives, Quickdraw and Horde

* **Thanos Fractured Frontier** installs player-owned Quickdraw state: six distinct shots, used/loaded flags and shot entity IDs (`AddGlobalLoadQuickdrawToPlayer`, 0x7cd6bdc; `LoadShot`, 0x7cd723c; `SelfLoadShot`, 0x7cd7ec8; `FireLoadedShots`, 0x7cd8054). **Archangel Horseman**, **Selene Horseman**, **Juggernaut Horseman** and **Stryfe** depend on objective progress or completion, which a restored position may not provide.
* **Zombie Venom** and other Horde effects rely on destruction events. `ZombieHordeAbilityDef::GrowZombieHorde` (0x7e0f96c) grows every owned Horde, not just the first. A new Horde is placed before growth, so growth can trigger effects in play.

## Restored-position coverage gates

Coverage is conditional: inclusion here does not mean every position containing the named entity fails. Strict advice declines when the relevant uncertainty can affect the position; approximate mode can continue with an approximation. Some information can be supplied through position history or adapter options. Below, the missing fields are grouped by the actual `coverage.rs` reasons, rather than promising an export for fields not present in the reader.

### Ability instances, text and pending triggers

* **Kang/Hulkling:** `OverrideAbilityCardDefId` and copied ability instance state; **High Evolutionary:** copied evolved ability identity; **Copycat**, **Nightmare**, **Red Onslaught**, generic copied effects: copied/generated ability identities. **Sauron:** modified hidden deck text; **Leech:** modified hidden hand text; generic text changes and **Patriot**: card text overrides or target client behavior definition. **Zero:** pending next-play trigger; **Weapon X Wolverine:** rage context.
* **Firehair:** prior destroyed-card resolve identities; **Rama-Tut:** prior randomly activated resolve identities; **Stryfe:** play-time On Reveal history and objective progress/completion. **Thanos Fractured Frontier:** `ShotsUsed`, `ShotsLoaded`, shot entity IDs. **Herbie:** current program and `ProgramsUsed` context. **Askanison:** dynamic cost increment history. **Merlin:** previous random spell index. **Champion:** `SpecialDeckDef` and `Card.StartedInDeck` provenance.
* **Jean Grey X-Men:** one-time `TriggerAbility` presence. **Hawkeye Avengers:** player `RepeatAddBuffAbilityDef` contexts `SourceCardEntityId`, `TurnToCheck`, `TargetLocation`. **Punisher War Machine:** draw-time `LocationEntityId` and `SourceCardEntityId`. **Vampire Deacon Frost:** `Player.StealsPerCardEntityId` recipient identities. **Sinister Six:** marked and played entity context. **Dormammu:** ritual source entity and merged state. **Galactus First Steps:** herald entity and trigger state. **Mysterio:** linked illusion entity.
* **Nebula:** installed pre-end trigger; **The Thing First Steps:** fill-trigger use; **Black Knight**, **Negasonic Teenage Warhead**, **Frankie Raye**, **Human Torch First Steps**, **Batroc**, **Hercules**, **Scream**, **Redwing**, **Quicksand**: relevant consumed trigger/use state. **Rescue:** pending proximity watch. **Supergiant:** pending concealment turns; **Snowguard Hawk:** remaining location-silence lifetime; **Invisible Woman First Steps:** activation multiplier. **Archangel Horseman**, **Selene Horseman**, **Juggernaut Horseman:** objective state. **Power Stone:** distinct played-stone history.
* **Phoenix Force/SP//dr:** attached movable ability; **Miek:** attached movement grant; **Arana:** pending next-card movement trigger; **Cloak**, **Hellion**, Champion Ghost Rider: player movement grant; **Peni Parker:** pending merge energy. **Spider-Man 2099**, **Nocturne**, **Rocket and Groot**, **Spider-Punk:** intrinsic movement consumption. **Hydra Stomper** and **Spider-Man 2099 Web-Slingers:** movement count history, including `PlayerMoveStats.NumberOfPlayerCardsMovedThisGame`. **Sparky:** last moved entity and previous zone. **Aunt May** and **Mary Jane:** current-turn resolved/moved-card history; **Miles Morales:** previous-turn forced movement.
* **Wave**, **Stick**, **Psylocke**, **Sandman**, **Black Swan**, **Baron Mordo**, Champion Doctor Doom/Invisible Woman/Herbie skills: potentially active player-owned cost or energy effects, target, source, attachment order, or resolved-action history. **Toxie Doxie:** locked hand identities; **Toxie Doxie Thunderbolts:** `CardTarget` next-turn player ability. **Kraglin** and **Djinn:** pending energy grant. **Electro**, **Wiccan**, **Arishem**, **Corvus Glaive**, **Fallen One**, **Havok**, and certain locations: prior permanent energy updates; **Arishem** additionally needs its pending turn-three trigger and initial random deck. **Cyclops X-Men:** `Player.HighestEnergyHadByPlayerThisGame`. Generic energy-spend-dependent effects need per-turn expenditure history.

* **Hope Summers:** current-turn queued energy grants. **Red Hulk Fractured Frontier:** draw history. **Kate Bishop:** pending arrow effects or hidden opponent arrows. **Malekith:** hidden target and game-end reveal attachment. **Lockjaw:** pending next-resolved-play swap.

### Cards, zones, actions and modifiers

* **Pixie:** original-deck provenance and shuffled hidden-deck costs; **Ten Rings:** hidden identity and upgrade; **Moira X** and **Sacred Timeline:** starting hand; **Loki:** target starting deck identities and replaced composition/costs. **Agamotto**, **Thor**, **Infinity Stones**, **Subterranea**, **Lechuguilla**, **Vibranium Mines:** post-shuffle deck composition. **Black Widow:** hidden Widow's Bite fate. **Darkhawk:** opponent deck count.
* **Agent Venom**, **Cassandra Nova**, **Vampire America Chavez**, **Anti-Venom Web-Slingers**, **Phastos**, **Mr Negative**, **Hydro-Man**: affected deck identities, power or cost modifications as applicable. **Monstro Octopus:** current cost, complete own-deck identities, top identity/cost, and a reconstructable cost basis. **Selene Brotherhood:** modified opposing hand power; **Valentina Thunderbolts:** generated hand identity/cost; **Scarlet Witch Brotherhood:** generated identity/power; **White Queen Brotherhood:** generated card and `LocationEntityIdKey`/`PlayerEntityIdKey` cost context.
* **Cobra**, **Quinjet**, **Victoria Hand**, **Panoptichron**, **Strange Supreme:** creation provenance or creation zone; **The Grand Design** and **Sentinel Champion:** created-card play history. **Khonshu** and destruction/discard-dependent effects require complete relevant zone inventories or event history. **Sanctum Infinitum:** `Player1TargetCardKey`, `Player2TargetCardKey` and discarded-versus-destroyed classification. **Viper**, **Annihilus**, **Green Goblin**, **Hobgoblin**, **Acid Arrow:** active `CanChangeCardOwnerHere` restriction.
* Last-play users need reconstructable identity, text and power; **Ghost Spider/Toad** and **Aero** need the respective seat's last-play history. **Leader:** current staged entities and previous zones. **Triton:** previous staged character order; **Brooklyn Bridge:** previous staged lane history; **Infinaut:** previous-turn staged plays; **Thena**, **Electro**, **Jean Grey**, **Lockheed**, **Doom 2099**, **Galacta**, **Chameleon:** relevant current-turn staging/play history. **Chameleon** also needs candidate reveal turns. **Invisible Woman:** concealed-card staging order. **Daredevil:** visible opponent plays. **Ego/Agatha:** `PostCreateStagingOptions` completion and staged-action undo state.
* **Ajax**, **Diamondback**, **Hospice**, **Scarlet Spider**: relevant modifier/stat-update history and, where needed, effective base power. Card augment attachments are not exported. Active-during-staging Ongoing may have ambiguous `stat_updates.updater_card_id` because it lacks ability-instance and Ongoing attribution; player and card cost-effect attachment order can also be missing. Graveyard cards' death lanes and discarded/destroyed provenance may be unknown.
* **Iron Fist** and **Enchantress** have state gates for revealed or previously seen sources. **Hawkeye** and **Jessica Jones** require their play turn and can be uncertain when Cosmo, Joaquin Torres or an amplified Joaquin affects the relevant prior-turn watch. Older unexplained face-down cards also trigger a state gate, except the identified Lemuria delay.

### Location state and generic unknowns

* **Feast/Altar of Death:** previous-turn location identities and pending player energy grants; a replaced location can hide such grants. `previous_turn_locations` accepts a complete per-lane identity history, but does not itself supply the pending grants. **Legion:** copied hidden-location identity and countdown. **Flooding**, **Hotel Inferno**, **Astral Plane**, **Fractured Frontier**, **Lemuria**, **Sakaar**, **Tinkerer's Workshop**, **Project Pegasus:** reveal timing when relevant. **Fractured Frontier** also needs draw-completed turn and bottom hand order.
* **Castle Zemo:** per-player betrayed state; **Krakoa:** prior consumption and affected deck-top power; **Aunt May's:** per-player movement-trigger consumption; **White Hot Room:** `IsGiven`; **Clubhouse:** per-player fill use; **The Abbey/Raft:** shared consumed trigger; **Vormir:** per-player consumption; **Time Theater:** per-player draw-use state; **Quantum Realm:** historical base-power assignment; **Otto's Lab:** spent state; **Collapsed Mine:** current action information to establish a skip; **Valley of the Hand:** complete destroyed-card graveyard. **Black Vortex:** per-player transformation flags.
* **White Hot Room** and **Sakaar Grand Prix:** prior permanent maximum-energy grants. **Mount Vesuvius:** retreat prohibition is not modelled, rather than an omitted reader field.
* Missing unsupported card/location rules, unknown face-down cards, unknown deck/opponent information, and unknown state are separate coverage outputs. For destroyed locations the adapter accepts `lane_layout: {"complete":true,"lanes":[entity_id,null,entity_id]}` only if all original lane identities were observed. It rejects incomplete or inconsistent layouts. See the README's live-state section for accepted inputs and limitations.

## Approximations and frozen-reference differences

The Rust client-rule path and the frozen Python oracle serve different purposes. Reference gates pin the old card/location pools (`parity/reference-pool.json`, `parity/reference-locations.json`), use `SNAPSIM_REFERENCE_RULES=1`, and compare plays-only plans. New-content positions are reported separately, not counted as Python parity. `cards/reference_rules.rs` retains deliberately changed old card rules; live advice does not enable it. The one explicitly recorded game divergence is spread seed 20261743, `$.turns[2].powers[2][0]` 5 → 6, because Horde grows all owned Hordes (`parity/divergences.json`). Unlisted and stale listed divergences fail that gate.

Deliberate client-path changes include the Ongoing pass order, staging preservation and conditional retries; selected copied/morphed resolve rather than all attached resolves; the 32-deep cap; Sinister London copying a banished staged Skill; Wolfsbane counting face-down allies; Yondu recording banishment; Zenn-La using printed cost and reveal; Shuri's Lab using the played lane; removed text no longer responding to events; hand/deck event dispatch; literal deck top; refresh before reveal callbacks; and movement/activation plans. The end-turn client path separates pre-end and end-turn dispatch instead of the frozen reference's per-card interleaving. These are implementation differences, not a claim that all client behavior has been proved.

Other documented limitations: merging power modifiers from the same source, all interdependent conditional Ongoing ordering, and gaps left by cards that cannot shift are not fully modelled. Visual-only Tao Mandala and Zombie Galacti attachments are omitted. `ReplacedCardDefId` follows copy/clear paths, but no implemented card replaces another. Live `LatestResolutionOrder` is used when exported, otherwise order is derived from played turn, priority and slot; recently exported live fields have not all been checked in live matches. Exact advice declines restored positions containing unregistered card or location IDs; explicitly enabled approximate advice can simulate catalog IDs as inert stand-ins. Hidden opponent data is sampled. A simulator replay or parity success only covers the exercised rules and evidence; it does not establish exact client RNG, full pools, or reconstruction of unobserved history.
